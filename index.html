Cozy Cat Café: Support

Cozy Cat Café is a cozy idle clicker for iPhone: tap to brew coffee, hire cat baristas, serve customers for combos and bonuses, and open new cafés for permanent boosts. This page has answers to common questions and tells you how to reach us.

Contact

Something not working, or an idea to share? Email contact@bokarsolutions.co.uk. It helps if you include:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About),
  • the app version (shown in the App Store),
  • what you were doing and what happened instead.

We read every message, but we are a small team, so replies can take a few days.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. The game works offline, and there is no sign-up.

Do my cats really earn while the app is closed?

Yes. When you come back you get a welcome-back bonus for up to 8 hours away, at half the normal rate. Sleepy cats earn double while you are away. Hyper cats only speed up while you are playing, because they depend on your combo.

Why are no customers coming?

Customers start arriving once you hire your first cat. They also wait while you are on the Cats or Location tab, so go back to the Café tab to serve them.

What do the drinks and bonuses do?

Each cat breed brews its own drink. A drink joins the menu when you own a cat of that breed. Serving a customer the right drink gives a bonus that depends on the drink (for example a tap boost, an income boost, a combo shield or a big tip). Serving the wrong drink, or letting a customer leave, resets your combo unless you have a shield.

What happens when I open a new location?

You start fresh, losing your coins and cats, but you keep your ⭐ reputation. Each ⭐ permanently boosts all income and taps by 10%. Each new location needs more coins earned in one run than the last.

Where is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new phone?

Your progress is saved only on your iPhone. There is no cloud account. If you restore a new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, the saved game normally comes with it. Deleting the app erases the saved game, and that is also how you start over from scratch.

How do I turn off the sound or vibration?

Tap the speaker icon at the top of the screen to mute the sound. Vibration follows your iPhone's System Haptics setting (Settings → Sounds & Haptics).

Can I replay the tutorial?

Yes. Tap the question mark at the top of the screen.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhones with iOS 17 or later. There is no iPad version yet.

Does the game collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy.
