Cozy Cat Café: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Summary

Cozy Cat Café does not collect, store on our servers, share, or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no connection to the internet.

What the app stores on your device

The app saves your game progress (your coins, cats, reputation, and settings such as sound on or off) on your own device so you can pick up where you left off. This data never leaves your device through the app.

If you use iCloud Backup, Apple may include this saved data in your device backup. That is handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy, and we have no access to it.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or usage data.

Third parties

The app does not include any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking tools, and it does not share any data with third parties.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, or notifications. It uses your device's speaker for sound effects and the haptic engine for vibration feedback, both of which can be turned off (the sound button in the app, and your device settings).

Children

Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we also collect none from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds features that change how data is handled (for example ads, analytics, or in-app purchases), we will update this policy and the app's App Store privacy information before releasing that version. The date at the top shows when this policy was last updated.
